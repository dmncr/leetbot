MILLISECONDS / CLOCK & LATENCY

Where does your clock stand?

Issued to {{ nick }} · one use · expires five minutes after your request. Keep the same nickname and IRC network.

  1. In mIRC, press Alt+R, choose Remote → File → New, then paste the script below and click OK.
  2. In the {{ channel }} window, run /leetclock_{{ token }}.
  3. The bot posts your result in the channel automatically. You can remove the script from mIRC afterward.

Uses mIRC COM and Windows PowerShell on Windows 10/11. It temporarily runs a hidden clock reader, closes it after the reply, and times out after two minutes. It does not change your clock.

Download .mrc ↗

The signed total timing difference comes first (your send timestamp minus bot receipt time), followed by the estimated Windows clock offset: positive means ahead, negative means behind. Round-trip latency excludes the bot’s reply queue. Windows samples have millisecond resolution. One-way transit and clock offset assume similar delay in both directions and include client handling overhead.
